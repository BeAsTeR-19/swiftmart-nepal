Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you provide to us, such as name, email address, phone number, and shipping address when you create an account or place an order.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Process and fulfill your orders
  • Communicate with you regarding your purchases
  • Improve our website and services
  • Send promotional emails (you can opt-out at any time)

3. Data Sharing and Security

We do not sell your personal information. We share necessary details with trusted partners (such as courier services like Pathao) solely for the purpose of fulfilling your order. We implement standard security measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure.

4. Cookies

Our website uses cookies to enhance your browsing experience, remember your preferences, and analyze site traffic. You can choose to disable cookies through your browser settings, but this may affect site functionality.

5. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal information. You can manage your account details through our portal or by contacting our support team.

6. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy periodically. We will notify you of significant changes by posting the new policy on this page.

If you have any questions or concerns about our privacy practices, please contact us at privacy@swiftmartnepal.com.
