About SwiftMart Nepal

We are a small business based in Kathmandu, dedicated to curating useful household products that are often hard to find locally. We started SwiftMart because we were tired of dealing with unreliable sellers, fake products, and hidden delivery fees on social media.

Our promise is simple: what you see is what you get. We only stock items that we would use in our own homes, ensuring decent build quality and genuine utility.

Whether you need to upgrade your kitchen, organize your desk, or make your living room cozier, we're here to deliver it to your doorstep reliably.

How to Order

  • Browse our catalog and Add to Cart
  • Proceed to checkout and enter delivery details
  • Choose Cash on Delivery, eSewa, or Khalti
  • Receive the package at your door

Delivery Information

  • Inside Kathmandu Valley: 1-2 days (Rs 100 fee)
  • Outside Valley: 3-5 days (Rs 200 fee)
  • Free Delivery: On orders over Rs 2,000 inside the valley!
